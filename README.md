# NYC Yellow Taxi Trip Data Analysis – 2023

## Project Overview

In this project, I analyse the **2023 New York City Yellow Taxi trip dataset** to explore travel patterns, passenger behaviour, revenue trends, and operational performance.

New York City taxis are an important part of the city's urban transportation network, operating in a highly dynamic environment where demand can vary significantly based on time, location, trip duration, and other factors. As an analyst working for an upcoming taxi operations company, my objective is to use data-driven analysis to understand these patterns and identify opportunities for improving operational efficiency and profitability.

Through **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**, I investigate the dataset to uncover meaningful patterns and answer key business questions such as:

- When and where is taxi demand the highest?
- How do trip patterns vary across different times of the day and days of the week?
- What are the typical trip distances and durations?
- Which factors have the greatest impact on taxi revenue and fares?
- How do passenger counts and payment methods vary across trips?
- Are there any unusual or potentially anomalous trip patterns?
- What insights can help improve taxi availability and operational efficiency?

The analysis involves **data cleaning, data quality assessment, univariate and bivariate analysis, outlier detection, feature exploration, and visualisation** to transform the raw trip data into actionable business insights.

The ultimate goal of this project is to use the findings from the 2023 taxi data to recommend strategies that can help a taxi operator **optimise fleet utilisation, respond better to demand, maximise revenue, reduce inefficiencies, and improve the overall passenger experience**.
